The Great AI Reality Check
Addressing your ongoing concerns, hype vs. reality, and what modern companies actually look for.

The Myth
"AI will completely replace human managers, financial analysts, and marketers, leaving no roles for fresh BBA graduates."
The Reality
AI replaces routine operational tasks, not strategic judgment, empathy, negotiation, or contextual leadership.
The Opportunity
The job market won't be "Humans vs. AI", but rather "Humans using AI vs. Humans not using AI."

Key Takeaway for BBA Students
