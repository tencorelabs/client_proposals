Solution Scope & Functional Agent Architecture

The proposed solution establishes a human-governed multi-agent operational platform to accelerate official communications, standardize public campaign distribution, adapt outdoor screen formats, and safeguard public trust against emerging misinformation.

Agent 1: Media Release Agent NLP & Translation
  • Ingests raw operational facts, police logs, and incident briefs.
  • Generates high-precision Arabic drafts adhering to official UAE government protocol and penal terminology.
  • Produces idiomatic, context-accurate English adaptations.
  • Auto-generates verified social captions, excerpts, and official campaign hashtags.
Agent 2: Campaign Creative Agent Ideation & Strategy
  • Generates structured creative concepts for public awareness (e.g., cyber safety, traffic rules, narcotics prevention).
  • Develops memorable slogans, key messages, and teaser sequences.
  • Structures comprehensive visual creative briefs for media production.
  • Aligns campaign messaging with seasonal events and Dubai Police goals.
Agent 3: Screen Adaptation Agent Visual Automation
  • Ingests approved master visual artwork across campaigns.
  • Maps assets against Dubai digital screens (DOOH, LED highway banners, station kiosks, portrait totems).
  • Enforces visual safe zones, legibility distances, and aspect ratios.
  • Produces multi-screen verification preview sheets for final sign-off.
Agent 4: Monitoring Intelligence Agent Sentiment & Defense
  • Continuously monitors authorized digital news streams and social feeds.
  • Performs sentiment classification and public safety topic tracking.
  • Detects velocity spikes in emerging rumors, fake news, or misinformation.
  • Generates structured situational alert dossiers and escalation summaries.
02 • Multi-Tier Human-Governed Validation Pipeline
1. Prompt Vault: Approved templates & RAG context
2. Safety Gateway: Azure AI Content Safety screening
3. Reviewer Check: Editorial & legal fact verification
4. Approver Gate: Authorized final sign-off & release

Technical Queries & Scope Clarifications (Part 1)

Key discussion points on AI capabilities, digital screen workflows, and external data dependencies.
TARGET: Dubai Police Communications & IT
FOCUS: Accuracy, Media Assets & Feeds
Category A: AI Generation, Linguistic Standards & Accuracy CRITICAL PATH
A.1 Arabic Dialect, Governmental Tone & Protocol Calibration
Dubai Police communications follow strict protocols regarding UAE government administrative phrasing, official rank honorifics, and exact penal code legal citations. Out-of-the-box LLMs typically default to generic Arabic.
Query: Will Dubai Police provide an official editorial style guide, terminology glossaries (prohibited vs. mandatory phrasing), and an archive of 500+ approved historical releases to calibrate the model's retrieval-augmented context (RAG)? Who within the communications team will act as the linguistic sign-off authority?
A.2 Definition and Measurement of the "97%+ Target Accuracy" Metric
Section 3.1 commits to a 97%+ accuracy rate. In generative natural language and creative copy, subjective editorial tweaks can blur the boundary of factual error versus stylistic preference.
Query: Is the 97% accuracy metric measured strictly on deterministic correctness (dates, locations, law article numbers, casualty figures, absence of hallucination) or on first-pass acceptance without wording revisions? What specific benchmark dataset will govern UAT sign-off?
A.3 Scope of Visual Output for Agent 2 (Campaign Creative)
The document specifies visual concept generation and teaser bundles, but Azure OpenAI (GPT-4o) natively produces structured text, not production-ready graphic compositions.
Query: Is Agent 2 expected to provide structured text briefs, storyboards, and prompts for human graphic designers, or should the platform directly render and export raster/vector image files using generative image models (e.g., DALL-E, FLUX, Stable Diffusion)?
Category B: Agent 3 (Screen Adaptation) Technical Scope MEDIA PIPELINE
B.1 Source Artwork Formats & Automated Rendering vs. Spec Guides
Digital out-of-home (DOOH) screens across Dubai vary widely across highway billboards, vertical portrait totems, and interactive kiosk resolutions.
Query: What are the primary input formats provided by your designers (e.g., layered PSD, Figma, AI, TIFF, or MP4/HTML5 video)? Is Agent 3 required to programmatically render and composite the final multi-resolution output files, or export dimensioned transformation preview sheets for designer execution?
B.2 Digital Signage CMS Integration
Distributing adapted screen assets to physical billboards typically relies on specialized signage software.
Query: Does Dubai Police utilize an existing central Digital Signage CMS (e.g., Scala, Broadsign, Samsung MagicINFO), and is direct API publishing to this network required, or will the platform package zip files for manual handoff?
Category C: Agent 4 (Monitoring Intelligence) & Data Licensing EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES
C.1 Access to Commercial Social Listening Firehoses & APIs
Sections 4.3 and 7.4 categorize third-party social listening platforms (Brandwatch, Sprinklr, Meltwater, X/Twitter Enterprise API) as customer-provided and excluded from vendor scope.
Query: Does Dubai Police currently hold enterprise subscriptions and active API keys for external listening feeds? If commercial APIs are unavailable, what specific authorized data sources (e.g., public RSS, official portal comment feeds) should Agent 4 ingest?
C.2 Misinformation & Rumor Detection Thresholds
Identifying false narratives requires baseline facts to compare against public posts.
Query: Will Dubai Police maintain an internal repository of verified facts for the agent to reference, or should alerts be driven strictly by statistical velocity anomalies and negative sentiment spikes across tracked keywords?
